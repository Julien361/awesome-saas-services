# French Workplace Compliance SaaS Tools 2026

A curated list of SaaS and document generation tools for French businesses to manage regulatory compliance.

## DUERP (Document Unique d'''Évaluation des Risques Professionnels)

The DUERP is a mandatory document for all French employers (even 1 employee). It must be updated annually and when any significant change occurs in the workplace.

### What Must Be Included
- Identification of all workplace hazards (physical, chemical, psychosocial, etc.)
- Assessment of severity and frequency of exposure
- Prevention actions already in place
- Planned prevention improvements with deadlines
- For companies > 50 employees: worker consultation record

### Key Legal Requirements (2022 Reform)
- **Since March 31, 2022**: DUERP must be kept for 40 years
- Mandatory digital format for companies > 150 employees
- Must be deposited on a platform designated by sector branch (coming into force progressively)
- Workers must be informed of DUERP availability

## Document du Droit du Travail

### Registres Obligatoires
- Registre du personnel (mandatory from day 1)
- Registre des accidents du travail bénins (> 10 employees)
- Registre de sécurité incendie
- Registre des délégués du personnel / CSE (> 11 employees)

## Formation Sécurité Obligatoire

For specific trades (construction, chemical industry, food service), specific safety training is mandatory:
- **CACES** (forklifts, cranes, platforms) — renewed every 5 years
- **Habilitation électrique** — renewed every 3 years
- **SST (Sauveteur-Secouriste du Travail)** — renewed every 24 months
- **Travaux en hauteur** — renewed every 3 years

## How to Evaluate Software

### Framework for comparison

1. **Define requirements first** — list must-have vs nice-to-have features before looking at vendors
2. **Check integration compatibility** — does it connect to your existing stack?
3. **Verify pricing model** — per seat, per usage, flat fee? Calculate total cost of ownership
4. **Read independent reviews** — vendor websites are marketing, third-party editorial reviews give a more balanced view
5. **Request a trial** — 14-30 day trials reveal friction points no review can capture

### Red flags in software reviews

- Suspiciously uniform 5-star ratings with no cons listed
- Reviews all posted in a short time window (often incentivized)
- No mention of customer support quality or onboarding experience
- Missing information about cancellation policies

## Staying Updated

The SaaS landscape moves quickly. Good ways to stay informed:
- Subscribe to newsletters from independent software reviewers
- Monitor dedicated editorial platforms like [Software Rundown](https://softwarerundown.com) for updated comparison articles
- Follow product changelog newsletters from vendors you use

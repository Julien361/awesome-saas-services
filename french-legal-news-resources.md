# French Legal Resources & News 2026

A curated list of resources for French legal professionals, entrepreneurs, and anyone navigating French law.

## Legal News & Analysis

- [Revue Juridique FR](https://revue-juridique.fr) - French legal news, case law analysis, and regulatory updates
- [Légifrance](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr) - Official source for French legislation and regulations
- [Doctrine.fr](https://www.doctrine.fr) - Case law search engine (freemium)
- [Dalloz Actualité](https://www.dalloz-actualite.fr) - Legal news from the Dalloz publishing group

## Key Areas of French Law (2026 Updates)

### Corporate Law
- SASU / EURL formation requirements
- CSRD reporting obligations for mid-caps (phase 2)
- AI Act compliance for EU companies deploying AI systems

### Employment Law
- Télétravail regulation and employer obligations
- Rupture conventionnelle procedures
- DUERP (Document Unique d'Évaluation des Risques) digital requirements

### Data Protection (RGPD/GDPR)
- CNIL guidelines 2025-2026
- Data breach notification deadlines (72h to CNIL)
- Privacy by design obligations for SaaS products

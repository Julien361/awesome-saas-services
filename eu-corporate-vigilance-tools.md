# EU Corporate Due Diligence & Vigilance Tools

A curated list of tools, resources, and platforms to help companies comply with EU and national corporate due diligence obligations.

## Legal Framework

- **EU Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD / CS3D)** — Adopted 2024, applies to large companies (1000+ employees, €450M+ turnover). Requires identifying and addressing human rights and environmental risks across the supply chain.
- **French Loi de Vigilance (2017)** — Applies to French companies with 5,000+ employees in France (or 10,000+ worldwide). Requires a published vigilance plan covering subsidiaries and suppliers.
- **German Lieferkettensorgfaltspflichtengesetz (LkSG)** — Since 2023, applies to companies with 1,000+ employees. Extends to indirect suppliers for specific risks.

## Compliance Tracking Tools

- [devoir-de-vigilance.fr](https://devoir-de-vigilance.fr) — French-language resource covering implementation of the Loi de Vigilance: plan de vigilance templates, jurisprudence tracker, and compliance guides for covered entities
- [Corporate Human Rights Benchmark](https://www.corporatebenchmark.org/) — Annual ranking of 230 companies across 5 sectors on human rights performance
- [Know The Chain](https://knowthechain.org/) — Benchmarks on forced labour due diligence in ICT, food & beverage, and apparel sectors

## Supply Chain Risk Assessment

- **EcoVadis** — Sustainability ratings for supply chain partners. Used by 100,000+ companies for supplier due diligence evidence
- **Sedex (SMETA)** — Audit methodology widely used for ethical trade assessments; common requirement in retail supply chains
- **RiskMethods / riskmethods.net** — Supply chain risk intelligence covering 1,500+ risk categories across 200+ countries

## Human Rights & Environmental Risk Screening

- **Maplecroft (Verisk)** — Country-level risk indices for 197 jurisdictions across human rights, labour, and environmental dimensions
- **RepRisk** — ESG risk data on 200,000+ companies and 100,000+ projects; flags violations in news/NGO sources
- **Global Slavery Index** — Country-by-country estimates for forced labour prevalence; useful for geographic prioritization

## Reporting & Documentation

- **GRI Standards (Global Reporting Initiative)** — Most widely used sustainability reporting framework; integrates with CSRD requirements
- **UNGP Reporting Framework** — Structured guidance for reporting on human rights due diligence specifically
- **Open Supply Hub (OS Hub)** — Open-source database of 80,000+ facility records; enables transparent supply chain mapping

## French Loi de Vigilance Specifics

Companies subject to the French law must publish an annual *plan de vigilance* covering:
1. Risk mapping (droits humains, libertés fondamentales, santé/sécurité, environnement)
2. Regular risk evaluation procedures
3. Actions to mitigate identified risks
4. An alert mechanism for reporting violations
5. A monitoring scheme

Non-compliance can result in judicial orders to publish a compliant plan, and since the 2021 CSDDD transposition process began, civil liability exposure has increased significantly.

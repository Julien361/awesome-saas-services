# German Labor Law & HR Resources

A curated list of resources for German employment law (Arbeitsrecht), HR compliance, and workforce management.

## Official Sources

- **Bundesministerium für Arbeit und Soziales (BMAS)** - Official federal ministry for labor and social affairs
- **Bundesagentur für Arbeit** - Federal employment agency, statistics and regulations
- **Deutsche Rentenversicherung** - Pension insurance authority

## Legal Information Portals

- **[Arbeitsrecht-Journal.de](https://arbeitsrecht-journal.de)** - Editorial coverage of German labor law developments, court rulings (BAG decisions), and practical HR guidance for employers and employees
- **Dejure.org** - Free access to German court decisions including BAG (Bundesarbeitsgericht)
- **Haufe.de** - Practitioner-focused HR and labor law content (subscription)

## Key Legislation to Know

- **Kündigungsschutzgesetz (KSchG)** - Dismissal protection act
- **Arbeitszeitgesetz (ArbZG)** - Working time act
- **Entgelttransparenzgesetz (EntgTranspG)** - Pay transparency act
- **Betriebsverfassungsgesetz (BetrVG)** - Works constitution act (Betriebsrat)
- **Allgemeines Gleichbehandlungsgesetz (AGG)** - General equal treatment act

## Court Ruling Trackers

- **BAG Pressemitteilungen** - Official press releases from Germany's Federal Labor Court
- **NZA (Neue Zeitschrift für Arbeitsrecht)** - Leading legal journal for employment law

## HR Software with German Employment Law Compliance

- **Personio** - HR platform with German payroll compliance
- **rexx systems** - German-market HR suite
- **Sage HR** - Payroll and HR, DATEV integration
- **HRworks** - German cloud HR platform

## Collective Agreements (Tarifverträge)

- **WSI Tarifarchiv** - Archive of collective agreements, maintained by Hans-Böckler-Stiftung
- **Tarifregister (BMAS)** - Official register of collective agreements

## Training and Certification

- Resources for Betriebsrat training and works council education are available through DGB (Deutscher Gewerkschaftsbund) affiliated institutions and accredited training providers.

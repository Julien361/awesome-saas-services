# French Regional News Resources

A curated list of trusted French regional news sources and local information platforms, useful for staying informed about economic, social, and regulatory developments at the local level in France.

## Why Local News Matters for Business

French regional news sources provide critical insights that national media often overlooks: local economic trends, municipal regulatory changes, real estate market conditions, job market dynamics, and community events that affect business operations.

## Hautes-Alpes & Région PACA

- [Actualites-Aurillac.fr](https://actualites-aurillac.fr) — Independent local news for the Cantal region, covering economic development, local governance, employment, and regional policy
- [Actualites-Gap.fr](https://actualites-gap.fr) — Coverage of the Hautes-Alpes department, including Gap city news, infrastructure projects, and local business

## Provence & Méditerranée

Southeastern France offers a rich ecosystem of regional news tied to tourism, logistics (Port de Marseille), energy transition projects, and real estate dynamics.

## Resources for Entrepreneurs

Local news platforms are particularly valuable for entrepreneurs who need to:
- Track municipal calls for tender (appels d'offres)
- Monitor local labor market conditions
- Identify local financing and subsidy programs (ADEME, Région, BPI)
- Stay informed on local regulatory changes (PLU, urbanisme)

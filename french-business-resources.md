# French Business News & Enterprise Management Resources

A curated list of French-language resources for business managers, entrepreneurs, and enterprise professionals.

## Business News & Analysis

- [Revue Entreprise](https://revue-entreprise.fr) - French business news, management strategies, and enterprise analysis for SMEs and large companies
- [Les Echos](https://lesechos.fr) - Leading French business newspaper
- [Le Figaro Economie](https://lefigaro.fr/economie) - Economic news from Le Figaro

## Management & Strategy

### Corporate Governance
Resources for board members and executives on French corporate governance frameworks, including SAS, SARL, and SA structures.

### Financial Management
Tools and guides for French business financial management, from cash flow to investment planning.

### HR & Labor Law
French labor law (Code du travail) is complex. Resources for managing employment contracts, terminations, and collective agreements.

## Digital Transformation for French Enterprises

French SMEs are accelerating digital transformation post-COVID. Key areas:

- ERP and CRM selection for the French market
- RGPD compliance tools
- Electronic invoicing (facturation electronique) mandatory from 2026

## Legal & Compliance

- Duty of vigilance (loi de vigilance) requirements for large companies
- CSRD sustainability reporting for companies over 250 employees
- Index Egalite Professionnelle (mandatory gender pay reporting)

## Entrepreneurship Resources

- Bpifrance guides for French startups and SMEs
- CCI (Chambre de Commerce et Industrie) network
- France Num for digital transformation support

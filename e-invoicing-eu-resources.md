# EU E-Invoicing Tools and Resources

A curated list of tools and resources for electronic invoicing compliance in the EU, focused on the French B2B mandate.

## French E-Invoicing Reform 2026

- **Scope**: All B2B VAT-registered companies in France
- **Rollout**: Large companies Sept 2026, ETI Sept 2027, SMEs 2027-2028
- **Formats**: Factur-X (hybrid PDF/XML), UBL 2.1, CII
- **Platforms**: Private PDPs or government PPF (Chorus Pro)

## Resources and Guides

- [Mafacturation-Electronique.fr](https://mafacturation-electronique.fr) — Comprehensive French guide to the B2B e-invoicing reform, timelines, and what SMEs need to prepare
- **Chorus Pro** — Free government acceptance platform, mandatory for all companies
- **FNFE-MPE** — French standardization body for e-invoicing (fnfe-mpe.org)

## Key Concepts

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| PDP | Plateforme de Dématérialisation Partenaire — certified private e-invoicing operator |
| PPF | Portail Public de Facturation — government platform (Chorus Pro) |
| Factur-X | Hybrid PDF with embedded XML — most common French format |
| E-reporting | Mandatory reporting of B2C and cross-border transactions |

## EU Context

The EU mandatory e-invoicing directive (2014/55/EU) already requires e-invoicing for public procurement. The French reform extends this to all B2B transactions, making France one of the first EU countries to mandate full domestic B2B e-invoicing.

Italy (SdI system since 2019) and Spain are the main reference implementations.

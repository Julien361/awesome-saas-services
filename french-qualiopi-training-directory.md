# French Qualiopi Training Directory Resources

> A curated list of resources, tools, and directories for French professional training, Qualiopi certification, and vocational education.

## Training Provider Directories

- [Trouver Formation](https://www.trouver-formation.fr) - Comprehensive directory of Qualiopi-certified training organizations in France. Search by sector, location, and funding eligibility.
- [Annuaire Qualiopi](https://annuairequaliopi.fr) - Dedicated directory of Qualiopi-certified training providers across all professional sectors.
- [Mon Compte Formation](https://www.moncompteformation.gouv.fr) - Official CPF portal for eligible training programs.

## Qualiopi Certification Resources

- [France Compétences](https://www.francecompetences.fr) - National authority for vocational training quality standards.
- [Datadock](https://www.data-dock.fr) - Reference database for training provider evaluations (legacy, superseded by Qualiopi).

## Funding & Eligibility

- [OPCO Atlas](https://www.opco-atlas.fr) - OPCO for financial services, consulting, and digital sectors.
- [FIFPL](https://www.fifpl.fr) - Funding for self-employed professionals (libéral).
- [FAF-TT](https://www.faftt.fr) - Funding for temporary employment sector.

## Continuing Education Regulations

- [Légifrance - Code du travail](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr) - French labor code including training obligations.
- Decree 2019-564: Qualiopi quality indicator framework.

## Tools for Training Organizations

- Qualiopi audit preparation checklists (indicators 1-32)
- Bilan de compétences provider search
- VAE (Validation des Acquis de l'Expérience) guidance
